## 🍺 The "nostalgia / why I made it" story post

A warm, personal origin story. Best posted on a quiet week, a milestone, or
just when you fancy it — these land best when they're not forced.

### Facebook version (page followers — warmer, emojis fine)

> A little story about The Valuator 🍺
>
> Years ago, when I was a much bigger drinker than I am now, I made an app to
> work out which beer was the best value — it was an iPhone app back then. Life
> moved on, the app lapsed, and these days you're more likely to find me out
> running or on the bike than down the offie.
>
> But I've always had a soft spot for it. So recently I rebuilt it from
> scratch — as a free web app this time, for everyone — partly out of
> nostalgia, partly just for the fun of making something again.
>
> I don't really need a beer value calculator any more. But if it helps you
> grab a better deal for the match, or stops you getting fleeced on a
> multipack, then brilliant — that's reason enough for it to exist.
>
> Cheers to old hobbies, new ones, and not overpaying for your beer. 🍻🏃🚴
> 👉 thevaluator.app

### r/uk_beer version (plainer, story-led, link in FIRST COMMENT)

**Title:** Bit of a confession about why I made that beer value calculator

> A few of you have seen the little per-pint calculator I made. Thought I'd
> share the daft backstory.
>
> I built the original years ago, as an iPhone app, back when I was a proper
> big drinker and genuinely wanted to squeeze the most beer out of my money.
> Then life changed — these days it's more running and cycling than real ale,
> and the old app quietly died.
>
> I rebuilt it recently mostly for nostalgia (and the fun of making something),
> as a free web app. I don't really need it myself any more, but if it saves
> someone a few quid on the match-day shop, that's a good enough reason to keep
> it going.
>
> Not a sales pitch — just a bit of a nostalgia trip. Cheers 🍻

*(First comment: `https://thevaluator.app`)*
